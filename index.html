01 · OVERVIEW

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to one mobile game developed by Leonardo Gabriel da Silva (publicly known as Leonardo Mondaine), which is published under two names:

InkfocusiPhone and iPad · Apple App Store

In this policy, "the app" means either version. Where the two versions work differently, this is stated explicitly.

The app does not have accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

02 · DATA WE COLLECT

Information Collection

  • Advertising data — The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data in order to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. See section 04.
  • Install attribution (Android only) — Color Match on Android uses AppsFlyer to measure which ad campaigns lead to installs. AppsFlyer may receive the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version and basic in-game events (for example, reaching a new high score or watching a rewarded ad). Inkfocus on iOS does not include AppsFlyer.
  • Game progress and focus statistics — Scores, high scores, settings, language, daily streak and the focus statistics described in section 03 are stored only on your device. We do not run servers for the app and this data is never uploaded to us.
  • Crash reports — If you have enabled crash and diagnostics sharing in your device settings, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports with us to help fix bugs.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect personal information directly.

03 · FOCUS STATISTICS

Focus Meter Data

The app measures your performance in each session to show your progress over time:

  • Reaction time, accuracy and Stroop effect (how much the meaning of a word slows you down when it does not match its color), per session.
  • Up to the last 400 sessions are kept, with the date, game mode, difficulty and score of each one.

These statistics are stored in a local file on your device. They are not sent to us, to advertisers or to any third party, and they are not linked to your identity.

The focus statistics are a training metric for entertainment. They are not a medical, psychological or diagnostic assessment.

04 · ADVERTISING & CONSENT

Google AdMob and Your Choices

The app is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC that is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland — where required, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. Reinstalling the app shows the form again so you can change your choice.
  • iPhone and iPad — the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android — you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information in ads.
