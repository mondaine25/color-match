01 · OVERVIEW

Introduction

✦ Family Friendly App

Color Match is a mobile game developed by Leonardo Gabriel da Silva (publicly known as Leonardo Mondaine). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play Color Match on Android devices.

Color Match is designed to be suitable for players of all ages, including children. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our players.

By downloading and playing Color Match, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02 · DATA WE COLLECT

Information Collection

Color Match is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to operate the game and serve advertisements.

  • Advertising data — Color Match uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and interaction data to serve relevant ads.
  • Game progress — Your score, high score, language preference, and difficulty settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not store this data on external servers.
  • Crash reports — Anonymous crash data may be collected automatically by the Android platform to help us fix bugs.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information directly.

03 · ADVERTISING

Google AdMob

Color Match displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

  • AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Ads shown in Color Match comply with Google's families policy and are appropriate for general audiences.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information, visit: policies.google.com/privacy

04 · CHILDREN

Children's Privacy

Color Match is a family-friendly game suitable for players of all ages. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with applicable children's privacy laws including COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • We do not allow behavioral advertising targeting children.
  • Ads shown to younger audiences comply with Google Play Families Policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.

05 · YOUR RIGHTS

Data Rights & Control

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:
